Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026
TL;DR: Notes & Tasks is a 100% offline app. Your notes, checklists, and tasks never leave your device. We do not collect, share, or sell any personal data.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Notes & Tasks ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information in our mobile application. Our application is designed with privacy as the absolute priority: it runs completely locally on your device and does not send any data over the internet.

2. Information Collection & Use

Because Notes & Tasks is an offline application, we do not collect any personal information, usage stats, or content that you create.

  • Local Storage: All notes, checklists, titles, dates, and settings are stored locally on your device's sandbox storage using standard platform persistence (SharedPreferences).
  • No User Account: You do not need to register, login, or connect any social media account to use the application.
  • No Cloud Sync: The app does not sync data to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party servers.

3. Permissions Required

The application operates with the minimal set of permissions required to function. It does not request permission to access your:

  • Contacts or Address book
  • Location (GPS)
  • Camera or Microphone
  • Device accounts or Identity

No special high-risk device permissions are required to use this application.

4. Data Security & Storage

Since your data is stored entirely on your own device, its security depends directly on your device's security:

  • We recommend keeping your device updated and secured using passcode, fingerprint, or face lock authentication.
  • Data Loss Risk: If you uninstall the application, clear the app data, or factory reset your device, all your saved notes and tasks will be permanently deleted. We cannot recover this data for you as we have no access to it.

5. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate any third-party services that track your usage, display advertising, or analyze user behavior:

  • No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or other tracking SDKs.
  • No third-party ad networks (like AdMob).
  • No social sharing libraries that track your identity.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are safe for children and do not address anyone under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect or store any information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
